### Simple Fitness & Yoga Checklist

* Include regular movement in your everyday routine.
* Choose activities that match your current fitness level and abilities.
* Combine different forms of activity when practical.
* Warm up appropriately before more demanding exercise.
* Pay attention to exercise technique and controlled movement.
* Include adequate rest and recovery between demanding sessions.
* Stay hydrated before, during, and after physical activity as appropriate.
* Increase exercise difficulty gradually rather than making sudden changes.
* Stop an activity if it causes unusual or significant pain and seek appropriate professional advice when needed.
