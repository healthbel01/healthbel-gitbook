### Simple Healthy Living Checklist

* Drink water regularly throughout the day.
* Include movement or physical activity in your daily routine.
* Choose balanced meals with a variety of nutritious foods.
* Keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule when possible.
* Take short breaks to relax and manage everyday stress.
* Make gradual changes that fit your lifestyle rather than relying on extreme routines.
