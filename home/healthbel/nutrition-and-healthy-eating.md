### Simple Healthy Eating Checklist

* Include a variety of foods rather than relying on the same choices every day.
* Add fruits and vegetables to meals and snacks when practical.
* Include an appropriate source of protein in your regular meals.
* Choose whole-grain and fiber-rich foods where suitable.
* Drink water regularly throughout the day.
* Pay attention to portion sizes and your individual hunger and fullness cues.
* Limit excessive reliance on highly processed foods and sugary drinks.
* Focus on sustainable eating habits instead of extreme or restrictive diets.
